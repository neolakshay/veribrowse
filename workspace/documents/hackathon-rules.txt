AI Day Hackathon Official Rules & Submission Guidelines

1. Overview:
   All teams must submit their final project details through the submission portal before the strict deadline.

2. Official Submission Page:
   URL: https://devpost.com

3. Requirements Checklist:
   - GitHub Repository (Must be public and include full code)
   - Project Description (Minimum 200 words covering features & verification)
   - Live Demo Link (Working application or demo environment)
   - Presentation Deck (PDF or Google Slides link)

4. Strict Deadline:
   20 September 2026, 6:00 PM IST (Late submissions will not be accepted)

5. Human Verification:
   The submission requires human confirmation before final submission to prevent accidental incomplete entries.
